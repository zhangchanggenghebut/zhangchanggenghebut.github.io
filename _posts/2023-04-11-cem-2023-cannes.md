Changgeng Zhang of Hebei University of Technology attended the 11th International Conference on Computation in Electromagnetics (CEM 2023), held in Cannes, France, on 11–14 April 2023. He presented the team’s research on intelligent electromagnetic computation and magnetic material modelling, and exchanged views with experts from China and abroad.

{% include news-photos.html group="cem_2023_transformer" %}

The electromagnetic field study explored data-driven estimation of magnetic field distributions in a simplified transformer model, comparing the effects of network architectures and material information on prediction performance.

## Sharing Research on Magnetic Material Modelling

The second presentation focused on ferrite magnetic characteristics at different temperatures. The study combined a recurrent neural network with the Play hysteresis operator to predict temperature-dependent hysteresis behaviour.

{% include news-photos.html group="cem_2023_ferrite_and_poster" %}

The conference combined brief presentations with interactive poster sessions. Through research presentations and on-site exchanges, Zhang shared ideas with colleagues from China and abroad and discussed developments in electromagnetic computation and magnetic material modelling.

## The CEM conference series

CEM began in London in 1992. Aachen, Wrocław and Edinburgh were among its later hosts before Cannes held the 11th meeting in 2023. The conference brings together researchers who use numerical methods to study electromagnetic fields, from low-frequency fields in electrical machines to high-frequency wave propagation. [Conference history](https://cem2025.com/)

Professor Alistair Duffy of De Montfort University chaired the Cannes meeting. He had served as president of the IEEE Electromagnetic Compatibility Society in 2020–2021. Professor Jan Sykulski of the University of Southampton chaired the international steering committee. [CEM 2023 committee](https://cem2023.com/committee) · [IEEE EMC Society past presidents](https://www.emcs.org/about-us/history/past-presidents/)

The Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET) co-sponsored CEM 2023. Writing in *IET Science, Measurement & Technology*, guest editor Cris Emson described CEM as the flagship meeting of the IET’s electromagnetics network. In 2024 the journal published extended versions of six papers from Cannes in a special section. Topics included permanent-magnet motor optimisation and the use of electromagnetic time reversal to locate partial discharges in power cables. [Guest editorial](https://doi.org/10.1049/smt2.12221)
