During Hebei University of Technology’s July 9–11 summer training program for administrators, Changgeng Zhang gave a talk on Moodle, the learning platform used at the university’s Finland Campus. Zhang, then deputy director of the Office of International Cooperation and Exchange, spoke at the session on international education.

He compared how Moodle, Rain Classroom, Xuexitong and D2L support course administration, academic integrity and the use of intelligent tools in teaching. He proposed linking the stages of course management in a single system to improve teaching quality and use instructors’ time more efficiently.
