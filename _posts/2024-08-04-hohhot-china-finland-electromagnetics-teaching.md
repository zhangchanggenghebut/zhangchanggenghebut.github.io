On August 4, 2024, Prof. Changgeng Zhang of Hebei University of Technology gave the first invited talk at the electromagnetics course forum in Hohhot, Inner Mongolia. Zhang serves on the council of China’s national society for electromagnetics teaching and textbook research. In his 9:00–9:30 a.m. talk, *Comparing Teaching Practices and Exploring New Approaches to Electromagnetics in China and Finland*, he drew on his teaching in Tianjin and at the Finland Campus to compare how the two courses use lectures, independent study and laboratory work.

## How students learn why fields fall off with distance

Zhang used point, line and surface sources to illustrate a difference between the textbooks he had taught from. He suggested pairing the reading with an experiment. Students could predict how field strength should change with distance for each source geometry, then test their predictions.

{% include news-photos.html group="hohhot_2024_reading" %}

The engineering electromagnetics course he described in Tianjin included two lab sessions. Its Finnish counterpart included 14 MATLAB programming labs. In one exercise on wave polarisation, students varied the amplitudes and relative phase of two electric-field components and watched the path traced by the tip of the resulting vector. Zhang also described how Moodle assignments and quizzes can give instructors a record of students’ progress throughout the course.

{% include news-photos.html group="hohhot_2024_labs" %}

## About the annual meeting

The 14th annual meeting on university teaching and textbooks for circuits, signals and systems, and electromagnetic fields took place in Hohhot on August 1–4. The two subject societies and Higher Education Press organised the meeting, which Inner Mongolia Agricultural University hosted. Nearly 300 teachers from close to 100 institutions attended. The programme included course forums, discussions of textbooks and simulation in the laboratory, a poster session and the finals of the sixth national teaching competition for early-career faculty in these subjects.
