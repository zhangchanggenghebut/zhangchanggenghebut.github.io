On May 6, 2025, Hebei University of Technology President Limin Wang led a delegation to Aalto University in Finland. Changgeng Zhang, then rotating rector of the Finland Campus, joined the visit alongside Yingying Zhao, director of international cooperation and exchange, Xiaojun Zhang, dean of undergraduate studies, and Mingzhe Chen, the campus’s student affairs officer and academic secretary.

Building on earlier student and faculty exchanges and research cooperation, the universities held their first formal leadership meeting. They discussed undergraduate and master’s curricula and how students can gain practical experience with companies. Research in robotics, new materials and sustainable energy also featured in the talks.

The delegation then visited Aalto’s robotics laboratory and the ePowerHub innovation platform to see work in smart manufacturing and sustainable energy and learn how it informs teaching across disciplines.
