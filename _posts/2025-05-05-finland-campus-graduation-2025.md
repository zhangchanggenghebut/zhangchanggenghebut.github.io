On May 5, 2025, Changgeng Zhang, then rotating rector of Hebei University of Technology’s Finland Campus, attended the campus graduation ceremony for the Class of 2025. Graduates and faculty from both universities gathered at LUT University in Lappeenranta, Finland.

A short film about the graduating class opened the ceremony. Two graduates then recalled what it was like to study in each other’s country. Xiyuan Zhang spoke about how her time in Finland shaped her understanding of innovation and friendship across cultures. Catherine Barnes recalled her exchange at Hebei University of Technology in Tianjin.
