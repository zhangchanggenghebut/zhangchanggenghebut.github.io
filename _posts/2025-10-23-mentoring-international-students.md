On October 21, 2025, Hebei University of Technology’s School of International Education invited Changgeng Zhang of the School of Electrical Engineering to share his experience supervising international students in China. Faculty from six schools took part in the workshop.

Drawing on years of advising students, Zhang discussed how to communicate across cultures, guide students’ academic work, put study plans into practice and review progress as graduation approaches.

{% include news-photos.html group="international_student_supervision_talk" %}

In the discussion that followed, faculty compared how they apply graduation requirements, track students’ academic progress and maintain standards across programs.
