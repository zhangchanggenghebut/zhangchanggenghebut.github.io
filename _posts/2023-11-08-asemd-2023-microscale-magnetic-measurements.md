Changgeng Zhang of Hebei University of Technology gave an invited talk on methods for measuring magnetism at the microscale at IEEE ASEMD 2023. His talk was part of the pre-conference tutorial program at Tianjin University.

{% include news-photos.html group="asemd_2023_presentation" %}

The IEEE International Conference on Applied Superconductivity and Electromagnetic Devices took place on October 27–29, 2023. Tianjin University and the IEEE Beijing Section co-hosted the meeting, which drew more than 250 participants. Other tutorials covered superconducting energy storage and rotational magnetization.
