On February 10, 2025, Changgeng Zhang, then rotating rector of Hebei University of Technology’s Finland Campus, welcomed a delegation from Saint Francis University in Hong Kong to Lappeenranta. The visitors included Provost Annie Bligh and business school dean Susana Yuen. Turo Kilpeläinen, president of LAB University of Applied Sciences, joined Zhang in welcoming them.

Zhang explained how the Finland Campus brings Hebei’s tradition of hands-on engineering education together with LUT University’s “Open Your Mind” approach. Bligh introduced Saint Francis University’s approach to teaching. The two sides discussed how to run joint education programs, and the visitors toured the campus to see where students study and spend time between classes.
