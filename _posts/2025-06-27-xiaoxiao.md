## Research brief

Electrical current crosses a worn interface through microscopic contact spots. The study examines how changes in surface topography affect the resistance measured across that interface.

### Surface measurements

The authors compared measured surface profiles with static contact resistance at different positions on a worn conductor. Fractal analysis described surface irregularities over multiple length scales. Changes in individual peaks and valleys help explain why two surfaces with a similar apparent contact area can conduct differently.

### Effect of contact load

Across the surfaces studied, the rougher profiles showed a stronger dependence of contact resistance on applied normal load. The distinction matters when comparing measurements: a resistance value is easier to interpret when the surface condition and the force used during the test are both recorded.
