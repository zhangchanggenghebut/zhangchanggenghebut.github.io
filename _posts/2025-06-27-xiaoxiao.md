## Research brief

Wear changes the small patches where two conducting surfaces actually touch. This study relates those changes in surface topography to electrical contact resistance.

The researchers examined how the geometry of worn surfaces is related to changes in electrical resistance. Two conductors that appear to touch across a broad area carry current through a smaller set of microscopic contact spots. Wear changes the number and distribution of those spots. The paper describes surface roughness across scales using fractal parameters and relates the observed surface condition to electrical contact behavior.
