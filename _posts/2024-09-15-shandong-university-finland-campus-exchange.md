On September 12, 2024, Hebei University of Technology welcomed a Shandong University delegation led by Xifeng Xu, head of its international affairs office. Vice President Hongyan Chen attended. The host university was also represented by Yingying Zhao, director of the Office of International Cooperation and Exchange, her deputies Yong Feng and Changgeng Zhang, and the staff of the office. Jian Zhou, director of development planning and dean of the Arizona College of Technology, and Gengshan Shi, dean of the College of International Education, also took part.

Zhang explained how the Finland Campus was established, what distinguishes its approach to teaching, and the principles that guide its day-to-day work. He also reported on the results of its work with students.

The visitors shared Shandong University’s experience with joint degree programs, support for international students and Chinese-language education. The two sides discussed challenges they had encountered. Hebei University of Technology expressed interest in sharing educational resources and expanding student exchanges.
