On April 15, 2025, Changgeng Zhang, then rotating rector of Hebei University of Technology’s Finland Campus, delivered the opening address at International Chinese Language Day in Lahti. He welcomed students, teachers and visitors who had come to learn more about the Chinese language.

Zhang encouraged students to listen to the rhythms of spoken Chinese and to explore Chinese history through music and dance. He invited the audience to consider what those traditions might mean to people living in different countries today.

{% include news-photos.html group="lahti_chinese_day_group" %}

Students and staff performed on the guzheng, a Chinese zither, and the hulusi, a gourd flute. Visitors could also try calligraphy, woodblock printing and painting Chinese opera masks. The Finland Campus hosted the event with support from LUT University.
