On May 7, 2025, a delegation from Hebei University of Technology visited Huawei’s research center in Helsinki, Finland. The delegation met with the center’s leadership to discuss research collaboration, closer links between industry and university education, and student training.

University President Limin Wang led the visit. He was joined by Yingying Zhao, director of the Office of International Cooperation and Exchange, Xiaojun Zhang, dean of undergraduate studies, Changgeng Zhang, then rotating rector of the Finland Campus, and Mingzhe Chen, the campus’s student affairs officer and academic secretary.

Huawei Finland was represented by Guoping Luo, head of technology planning, Yu Qi, the center’s human resources director, and Yuqiuge Hao, manager for technical cooperation.

Translated from the report published on WeChat by the international office of Hebei University of Technology on May 11, 2025.
