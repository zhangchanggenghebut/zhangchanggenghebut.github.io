A double-yoke fixture and laser vibrometer resolve the small cyclic strains that contribute to vibration in high-frequency transformer cores.

We measured dynamic magnetostriction in 1K107B nanocrystalline laminations using symmetric magnetic yokes and a mirror that folds the laser beam into an L-shaped path. An energy-based model predicts the strain, and a particle-swarm-optimized LSTM corrects its residual error. Under sinusoidal excitation at 5 kHz and 0.5 T, this correction reduced root-mean-square error by 49.64% relative to the energy-based model alone.

### 纳米晶叠片高频磁致伸缩的测量与预测

磁致伸缩是高频变压器铁芯的振动源之一，双磁轭配合激光测振可测出叠片在一个周期内的微小伸缩。

我们用对称双磁轭和镜面折转的 L 形光路，测得 1K107B 纳米晶叠片的动态磁致伸缩曲线。先用能量模型计算应变，再用粒子群算法优化的长短期记忆网络修正预测偏差。在 5 kHz、0.5 T 的正弦激励下，耦合模型的均方根误差比单独使用能量模型降低了 49.64%。
