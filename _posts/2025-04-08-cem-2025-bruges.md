Professor Changgeng Zhang of Hebei University of Technology attended the 12th International Conference on Computation in Electromagnetics (CEM 2025), held in Bruges, Belgium, on 8–11 April 2025. He presented his research and exchanged views with experts from China and other countries.

{% include news-photos.html group="cem_2025_talk" %}

On 10 April, Professor Zhang presented “Measurement and Application of the Magnetostriction of Oriented and Non-Oriented Electrical Steel Sheets” in Session P7. He introduced an L-shaped optical measurement method and neural-network modelling. Using an anode saturable reactor as a case study, he examined the effects of material anisotropy and magnetostriction on core vibration.

## Academic Exchange with Experts from China and Abroad

After the presentation, Professor Zhang took part in a question-and-answer session and discussed research ideas with participants. He also met colleagues from China and abroad during the conference to share research progress and learn about one another’s work.
